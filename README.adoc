= Echo Types — Glossary
:toc: preamble
:toc-title: Contents
:icons: font
:doctype: article

Cross-reference glossary for link:README.adoc[README.adoc], link:EXPLAINME.adoc[EXPLAINME.adoc], and the `echo-types` wiki. Terms are classified as **standard** (existing mathematical concept used without modification), **rephrased** (standard concept given project-specific vocabulary), or **project-specific** (originating in this project).

== Core concepts

[[echo]]
Echo::
  Given `f : A → B` and `y : B`, the echo at `y` is the homotopy fiber `Echo f y := Σ (x : A), (f x ≡ y)`.
  *Classification:* **rephrased**. The mathematical object is the standard preimage/homotopy fiber. "Echo" is project vocabulary emphasising the witness-of-loss reading.
  *See also:* <<semantic-fibre>>, <<fiber-total>>

[[semantic-fibre]]
Semantic fibre::
  The structured set of possible typed origins lying over an observed artefact under a declared non-injective map.
  *Classification:* **rephrased**. Mathematically identical to the preimage fiber or homotopy fiber. The "semantic" qualifier distinguishes from CS fibers (lightweight threads) and network fibre, and signals the emphasis on retained information structure.
  *See also:* <<echo>>, <<avec-fibre>>, <<sans-fibre>>

[[avec-fibre]]
Avec fibre::
  An artefact that carries, or is accompanied by, enough semantic fibre to support the inferences or uses being made.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.
  *Antonym:* <<sans-fibre>>

[[sans-fibre]]
Sans fibre::
  An artefact that is only a valid target-side value: no meaningful possible-origin structure is retained or declared.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.
  *Antonym:* <<avec-fibre>>

[[echo-type]]
Echo type::
  The dependent type `Echo f : B → Type` assigning to each `y : B` the fiber `Σ (x : A), (f x ≡ y)`.
  *Classification:* **rephrased**. The type family is the standard fiber type family. "Echo type" is project vocabulary.

[[warrant]]
Warrant::
  The licence or permission that an echo/fibre gives for an inference or operation. A warrant is what the retained fiber structure *enables*; it is not itself a first-class object in the formal kernel.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.
  *Usage note:* The public surface talks about echoes and fibres rather than making warrant the headline term.

== Structural objects

[[fiber-total]]
Fiber total space (Σ B (Echo f))::
  The dependent sum `Σ (b : B), Echo f b` — the total space of the fiber fibration over `B`.
  *Classification:* **standard**. Equivalent to the domain `A` by the total-space-of-fibers fact (HoTT Book §4.8).
  *Previous name in this repo:* `Image f` — a misnomer, since the HoTT image requires propositional truncation of fibers. Renaming to `FiberTotal f` is pending.

[[domain-replacement-factorization]]
Domain-replacement factorization::
  The factorization `A ≃ Σ B (Echo f) → B` where the left leg is an equivalence and the right leg is a projection. Called "domain replacement" because the middle object is equivalent to `A`.
  *Classification:* **rephrased**. The factorization is a standard consequence of the total-space equivalence.

[[echo-total-completion]]
Echo total completion::
  The equivalence `A ≃ Σ B (Echo f)` — the Tier-1 headline theorem.
  *Classification:* **rephrased**. Standard total-space-of-fibers fact (HoTT Book §4.8), restated in Echo vocabulary.
  *Previous name in this repo:* "slogan-unlock" — rhetorical language that did work the theorem didn't support. Removed.

[[image-hott]]
Image (HoTT sense)::
  `Σ (b : B), ∥ fib f b ∥₀` — the propositional truncation of the fiber, giving the smallest subobject of `B` through which `f` factors as a surjection followed by an embedding.
  *Classification:* **standard**. Distinct from <<fiber-total>>. The repo does not define or use this construction.

== Factorization systems

[[wfs]]
Weak factorization system (WFS)::
  A pair `(L, R)` of classes of maps such that every map factors as `L` followed by `R`, and diagonal lifts exist against `L` from the left and `R` from the right.
  *Classification:* **standard**.
  *Current status in this repo:* The (equivalence, projection) factorization forms a WFS. Proved in `EchoOrthogonalFactorizationSystem.agda` (module name overclaims OFS; rename pending).

[[ofs]]
Orthogonal factorization system (OFS)::
  A WFS where diagonal fills are *unique* (equivalently: the solution space is contractible).
  *Classification:* **standard**.
  *Current status in this repo:* Not yet proved. The gap between WFS and OFS is the most important open technical task for the factorization story.

[[ofs-witness]]
ofs-witness::
  The term witnessing factorization existence + fiber identification in `EchoOrthogonalFactorizationSystem.agda`.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (name). The witness is for a *weak* factorization system. Rename to `wfs-witness` is pending.

== Loss and residue

[[structured-loss]]
Structured loss::
  Information loss under a non-injective map where the fiber over each output retains a proof-relevant constraint on what was lost — loss that is not total erasure.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (as a defined term). The phenomenon is standard; the emphasis is project-specific.

[[residue]]
Residue::
  What lower-level evidence remains after a degradation. In the Echo formalism: a lossy observation of the fiber, coarser than the full echo but still informative.
  *Classification:* **rephrased**. The word "residue" has prior use in graded modalities and linear logic. Here it specifically means a weakened/observed fiber, not the algebraic or complex-analysis concept.

[[degrade]]
Degrade::
  The operation that weakens a full echo to a residue, losing information in a controlled way. Satisfies `degrade-id` (degrading by the identity grade is identity) and `degrade-compose` (degrading by a composite grade is degrading by each in sequence).
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (as a named operation with these laws). The pattern is standard in graded modality systems.

[[no-section]]
No-section theorem::
  A proof that a collapsing (non-injective) map `f` has no section — there is no `s : B → A` with `f ∘ s = id`. This is the formal witness that the loss is irreversible.
  *Classification:* **standard** (consequence of non-injectivity in dependent type theory).

== Classification

[[loss-taxonomy]]
Loss taxonomy::
  The four-axis function-side classification: EQUIV (no loss), INJ (loss only from domain restriction), SURJ (loss only from identification), CONST (total loss).
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (as a mechanised grid). The classification itself is standard; the mechanisation and Echo-vocabulary framing are project-specific.

[[residue-taxonomy]]
Residue taxonomy::
  The four residue forms classifying the shape of information that survives after degradation.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

[[decoration-structure]]
Decoration structure::
  A seven-field recipe specifying how a type is decorated with Echo information, including the degrade operation and its laws.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

[[observational-equivalence]]
Observational equivalence (≡m)::
  Mode-indexed equality on echoes: two echoes are observationally equivalent at mode `m` if they agree under all observations available at that mode.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

== Separation and identity

[[matched-negative]]
Matched-negative::
  A mechanised proof that a candidate construction (Shannon entropy, LL encoding, resource measure) does *not* capture Echo structure — either by showing it fails to distinguish distinct echoes, or that equal observations under the candidate don't imply equal echoes.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (methodology). Proving your object isn't its obvious neighbours is how identity claims get earned; most projects don't bother.

[[identity-claim]]
Identity claim::
  The claim that echo types are a concept with their own identity, not just a repackaging of standard fiber machinery. Evaluated against three falsifiability criteria: (1) distinct phenomenon, (2) characteristic theorem family, (3) canonical examples.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

[[falsifiability-gate]]
Falsifiability gate::
  A specific, mechanisable condition whose failure would refute the identity claim. Recorded in `roadmap-gates.adoc`.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

[[retraction-ledger]]
Retraction ledger::
  A living document recording every retracted claim, when it was retracted, the counterexample or obstruction that forced retraction, and the narrowing (what is still true).
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (as a formalised practice). The concept of recording retractions is not new, but mechanising it as a first-class project artifact with earn-back gates is unusually disciplined.

== Audience surfaces

[[audience-surface]]
Audience surface::
  A module packaging Echo vocabulary for a specific domain audience (provenance, security, probabilistic, differential). Each ships: a record type, parametric headline theorems, a worked instance, and matched-negatives (honest-bound disclosure).
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

[[security-surface]]

  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

[[probabilistic-surface]]
Probabilistic-support surface::
  The audience surface for sampling/draw-id use cases. `EchoProbabilisticSupport.agda`.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

[[differential-surface]]
Differential surface::
  The audience surface for sensitivity/perturbation-tracking use cases. `EchoDifferential.agda`.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

== Foundation contract

[[foundation-contract]]
Foundation contract::
  The stable API surface (`Echo.*` namespace + `FOUNDATION_CONTRACT.md`) that downstream languages depend on, isolating them from research sprawl.
  *Classification:* **project-specific**.

[[anti-collapse-separation]]
Anti-collapse separation::
  The mechanised invariant that Echo is not a resource instance: a resource algebra may measure Echo residues, but it does not define Echo. Equal residue measure does not imply equal Echo.
  *Classification:* **project-specific** (theorem). The content is a specific negative result about Echo's relationship to resource algebras.

== Distinctions from nearby concepts

[cols="1,2", options="header"]
|===
| Term | Says what?

| Provenance
| *Where* something came from

| Trace / lineage
| *How* it got here

| Residue
| *What lower-level evidence remains* after degradation

| Semantic fibre
| *What possible origins still lie over* the observation
|===

The useful formulation is not "loss became total erasure" but "loss left a declared fibre, and that fibre may or may not be informative enough for the intended use."
