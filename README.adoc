== Overview

Most formalisms recognise two cases: reversible maps (no important loss) and irreversible maps (loss occurs and is usually forgotten). Echo types target a third case: **irreversible maps where the fiber over each output retains a proof-relevant constraint on what was lost**.

Given `f : A → B`, the echo at `y : B` is the homotopy fiber:

    Echo f y := Σ (x : A), (f x ≡ y)

This is the standard preimage fiber, not a new construction. What this repo contributes is:

* **Emphasis** — treating fibers as first-class structured-loss witnesses rather than bookkeeping artifacts.
* **Taxonomy** — a mechanised classification grid of loss kinds × residue shapes.
* **Separation proofs** — matched-negatives proving Echo is not reducible to Shannon entropy, linear-logic encodings, or resource grades.
* **Audience surfaces** — provenance, security, probabilistic, and differential views, each with honest bounds.
* **Honesty infrastructure** — a living retraction ledger recording every retracted claim, its counterexample, and its narrowing.

Whether emphasis-plus-taxonomy-plus-separation constitutes a distinct concept with its own identity is the question this repo is designed to falsify, not assert.

== The factorization

Every map `f : A → B` factors through its total fiber space:

                  encode f
        A ────────────────────→ Σ B (Echo f)
         ╲                            │
          ╲                           │ proj₁
         f ╲                          │
            ╲                         │
             ╲                        ↓
               ────────────────────→ B

Left leg (encode f)::
  EQUIVALENCE — the standard total-space-of-fibers fact (HoTT Book §4.8), restated in Echo vocabulary. Proved in `EchoTotalCompletion.agda`.

Right leg (proj₁)::
  PROJECTION — forgets the fiber witness.

Triangle::
  `f ≡ proj₁ ∘ encode f` — pointwise definitional unconditionally; strict at the function level given funext (F5-1).

This is the **domain-replacement factorization**: the middle object `Σ B (Echo f)` is equivalent to `A`. It is *not* the image factorization in the HoTT sense, which requires propositional truncation of fibers. The distinction matters: an HoTT reader encountering `Σ B (Echo f)` under the name `Image f` will correctly identify it as the domain in disguise. We call it what it is.

== Known technical gaps

[CAUTION]
====
**WFS, not yet OFS.** Diagonal lifts exist (F5-2) and factorisation is unique up to iso (F5-3), giving a *weak* factorization system. Unique diagonal fills — the requirement for orthogonality — are not yet proved. The module name `EchoOrthogonalFactorizationSystem` reflects the target, not the current achievement. Renaming to `EchoWeakFactorizationSystem` is pending.
====

[CAUTION]
====
**Module naming debt: `EchoImageFactorization`.** The middle object `Σ B (Echo f)` is equivalent to the domain, not the HoTT image. Renaming to `EchoFiberTotalFactorization` is pending.
====

[CAUTION]
====
**Positive identity is open.** Matched-negatives establish that Echo is *not* its obvious neighbours. The falsifiability criteria also require a *characteristic theorem family* — results that are naturally Echo-shaped, not just generic fiber lemmas. Whether emphasis + vocabulary + separation suffices for identity is the question the repo is designed to answer.
====

[CAUTION]
====
**funext is an explicit module parameter for Tier-3 results.** The unconditional fragment under `--safe --without-K` covers factorization existence and fiber identification only. The strict triangle, diagonal lifting, and factorisation uniqueness require funext supplied by the caller.
====

== Semantic fibre vocabulary

Echo Types are for cases where the value that crosses a boundary is no longer the original guarantee, but is also not meaningless. They are *not* a replacement for ordinary type checking, ABI proofs, FFI discipline, or any boundary system that can preserve exact guarantees. When exact preservation is available, use it.

[horizontal]
Semantic fibre:: The structured set of possible typed origins lying over an observed artefact. Mathematically: the preimage fiber `Echo f y`.
Avec fibre:: The artefact carries enough semantic fibre to support the inferences being made.
Sans fibre:: The artefact is only a valid target-side value; no possible-origin structure is retained.
Echo:: The surviving transformed signal or constraint after loss.
Warrant:: The licence that an echo/fibre gives for an inference or operation.

Distinctions from nearby concepts:

[horizontal]
Provenance:: says *where* something came from.
Trace / lineage:: says *how* it got here.
Residue:: says *what lower-level evidence remains* after degradation.
Semantic fibre:: says *what possible origins still lie over* the observation.

The useful formulation is not "loss became total erasure" but "loss left a declared fibre, and that fibre may or may not be informative enough for the intended use."

== What Echo Types Are For

Echo types are useful when outputs are:

* insufficient to reconstruct their source exactly
* still sufficient to constrain the source non-trivially

Intended proof-use cases: non-injective computation, provenance, structured irreversibility, partial recoverability, classification up to equivalence, forensic inference from residues, refined taxonomies of information loss.

=== Who is this for

* Authors of formally-verified protocols where role projections, privacy collapses, or lossy aggregations need typed witnesses for what was retained.
* Authors of refinement-typed and graded type systems looking for a foundational vocabulary for loss-with-residue.
* Anyone designing audit, provenance, or hash-chain systems who wants a proof-relevant account of what the audit step preserves.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Echo is *not* a resource instance. A resource algebra may measure Echo residues, but it does not define Echo: equal residue measure does not imply equal Echo (mechanised: `equal-measure-does-not-imply-equal-echo`, `measure-not-injective`). Do not model Echo as a `Soundness(S)` resource-algebra instance. Resource grade ≠ echo index ≠ residue measure; avoid "echo-grade".
====

== Identity Claim and Falsifiability

This repo is trying to establish echo types as a concept with its own identity. Since Echo is built from sigma/fiber machinery, identity will not come from syntax. It must come from role and theorems.

The claim is established only if all three are met:

. **Distinct phenomenon** — structured loss under non-injective computation.
. **Characteristic theorem family** — results that are naturally echo-shaped, not just generic sigma lemmas.
. **Canonical examples** — cases where echo type is the right explanatory unit.

If these fail, we record that result and stop the identity claim.

Criterion 1 is supported by the matched-negatives (Echo is not entropy, not LL encoding, not a resource grade). Criterion 2 is open: the Tier-1 theorem is standard, and the remaining theorems are standard consequences of non-injectivity restated in Echo vocabulary. Criterion 3 is supported by the audience surfaces and tutorials. The repo is structured to make the gap in criterion 2 visible and falsifiable.

agda -i proofs/agda proofs/agda/Echo.agda

== Installing as a library

[source,bash]
----
git clone https://github.com/hyperpolymath/echo-types.git
echo "$(pwd)/echo-types/echo-types.agda-lib" >> ~/.agda/libraries
----

Then in your project's `.agda-lib`:

[source]
----
name: my-project
depend: standard-library echo-types
include: src
----

Requires standard-library v2.3 or later registered alongside.
