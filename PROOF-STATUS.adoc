== Identity and adjacency: EI-2 disposition

* *Closed negatively — EI-2, PATH B.* The claim that the existing
  decoration-order / echo-indexed-family / transport recipe supplies
  substantive *simultaneous cross-axis interaction* as a Gate 1
  distinctness argument did not survive the investigated cases. The
  seven recorded data points and the `RoleRole` structural argument
  support that verdict; they are not a universally quantified no-go
  theorem. The generic 2D iff remains unproved. See `docs/EI2_REPORT.adoc`.
* *Narrowed, not retracted wholesale.* Gate 1's remaining arguments
  concern proof-relevant witnesses versus specified propositional or
  single-witness accounts, and the cited quotient/Galois 2-cell
  constructions. These are neighbour- and model-specific, not proof that
  the fibre is a novel construction. `Echo f y` is definitionally a
  fibre; bare Echo coincides with HoTT fibres and Σ-relevant refinements.
* *Open and separate.* The adequacy of a characteristic theorem family
  for an overall positive identity remains contested: README criterion 2
  remains unmet, while the historical Gate 2 audit records a narrower
  protocol pass (`docs/characteristic.adoc`). They are not the same
  standard. Specific encodability/comparison questions against QTT and
  modal calculi, type-theoretic IFC, and the Galois/quotient settings
  remain, as do any coupled-state or multiple-live-position extensions;
  none reopens EI-2 or earns back the rejected distinctness claim merely
  by encoding or failing to encode `RoleGraded`.
