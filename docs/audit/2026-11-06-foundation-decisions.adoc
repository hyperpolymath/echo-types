== Research questions that are not routine proof repair

* A minimal-residue theorem must specify the representation class, its
cost measure, and whether domains are finite/decidable. A retained source
witness is sufficient for recovery; it is not automatically minimal.
* Enforced consumption requires an operational or typing contract, such
as opaque handles and a heap transition system. The present pure Agda
cell permits retaining an earlier holding value. A Rust/Idris refinement
needs an identified implementation revision and a simulation relation.
* A tropical claim must give the complete index category, tensor/unit,
infinity behavior, grade-change direction and coherence laws. The new
natural-number counterexample rules out using loss alone as an obstruction.
* Priority cannot be inferred from successful typechecking or internal
gate labels. The kernel is a standard dependent fibre; a proposed new
contribution needs a precise additional theorem or an evaluated application
advantage and a primary-literature comparison.

The coordinator retains responsibility for theorem meanings and these
research decisions; the bounded packets delegate implementation, not
permission to strengthen claims.
