== Checked local declarations and their scope

`EchoSourcePreserving` proves that a source-preserving fibre isomorphism with identity fibres yields pointwise identity, and constructs the converse with its pointwise inverse laws. Boolean negation supplies the counterexample to unrestricted fibre equivalence as an identity criterion.

`EchoCNOSemantics` ties a stored provenance witness and certificate to the same program. It relates native nullness using `CNO.state-eq` to `IsCNO` within the pinned dependency's total, zero-energy model, and supplies a Nop negative control. Its `SourcePreservingStateMap` criterion characterizes native nullness using a source-preserving *map of relation-valued fibres*. That record does not assert the two inverse laws of `EchoSourcePreserving.FibreIso`; the two interfaces must not be conflated.

`EchoDependentPushforward` proves family hom-setoid category laws, pushforward/reindexing action laws, a natural hom correspondence, unit/counit triangles, a terminal-family fibre comparison, and a natural isomorphism for composition. The equality on homs is pointwise, without a global funext or UIP axiom. The composition comparison is not a full proof of coherence for an arbitrary base-indexed pseudofunctor or fibration package.

`EchoGradedComonadNaturality.NaturalGradedComonad` supplies the missing pointwise congruence and naturality obligations as a companion preserving the existing record. The F1, natural-grade, and list-grade witnesses pass their local checks. Grade-local UIP for natural/list transports is not a global source-type UIP principle. `EchoNaturalityIndependence` provides the bounded countermodel motivating the extra interface fields.

`EchoErasingGradedComonad` checks a natural graded comonad whose grade-zero carrier is `A` and positive-grade carriers are unit, including a non-injective erasure witness. Its ordinary natural-order degradation maps and compatibility equations are also accepted, subject to the Cubical transport warnings above. This refutes a blanket impossibility of graded comonads involving information loss. It does not identify the construction with an intended tropical Echo model or recover erased sources. `EchoThinPreorderExample` confirms that the abstract thin-preorder record need not satisfy antisymmetry.

The choices and bounded research packets are recorded in `2026-11-06-foundation-decisions.adoc`. No new linear runtime or ordered tropical programme is implied by these repairs.
