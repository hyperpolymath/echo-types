= Echo types — object and contribution boundary
:toc: macro

== The object

For `f : A → B`, `Echo f y := Σ (x : A) , (f x ≡ y)` is the standard dependent fibre. It is a derived Σ-type with no new type former, judgment, or general separation from dependent pairs. `Echo.agda` supplies the definition and ordinary fibre laws; `EchoFiberTriangulation.agda` relates it to standard-library surjection witnesses and reverses the equality orientation.

An inhabitant contains an actual source `x`, together with evidence that `f x ≡ y`. The total-space equivalence `A ↔ Σ B (Echo f)` in `EchoTotalCompletion.agda` retains that source. It has no loss-grade parameter and proves neither recovery from `y` alone nor minimal storage of a complement.

== Additional interfaces

`Echo.Index.ThinPoset.ThinPoset` has reflexivity, transitivity, and propositional homs. Its abstract fields omit antisymmetry: mathematically it is a thin preorder, despite its compatibility name. The concrete three-element `keep ≤ residue ≤ forget` instance is an order.

`Echo.Modality.Interface.EchoModality` specifies a family of types and an identity/composition-preserving action over that index. It is a preorder-indexed diagram. The record supplies no reflector, reflective-subuniverse predicate, idempotence, or modal elimination principle. The word “modality” is project vocabulary rather than a proved reflective modality.

`EchoSeparating` distinguishes parallel arrows acting by identity and Boolean negation. The refuted law identifies a chosen composite with an arbitrary parallel arrow. Ordinary composition with the chosen composite is compatible with these different actions; the example does not refute functor composition.

== Recovery and variance

The historical identifier `no-section-of-collapsing-map` negates a *left inverse*: no `raise` satisfies `raise (lower x) ≡ x` when `lower` collapses two distinct inputs. It does not negate a categorical section (a right inverse). `Bool → ⊤` has the two constant right inverses selecting `true` and `false`.

`EchoVariance.agda` gives inverse maps between a composite fibre and the corresponding iterated fibre for a supplied factorisation. Those maps do not provide a full tropical graded monad: the endofunctors, grading coherence, naturality, and all monad laws require a separate construction. An erasing grade cannot generally recover arbitrary source values; the exact obstruction must name its carrier and counit. This does not imply that every loss-related graded comonad is impossible. Experimental writer carriers retain content and can support both structures. The baseline `GradedComonadStructure` record omits naturality. The local `NaturalGradedComonad` companion and its witnesses pass Agda 2.7.0.1 checks. An erasing graded-comonad counterexample also passes; its ordered compatibility equations have the Cubical transport warning boundary recorded in the evidence ledger.

== Contribution and outstanding evidence

The contribution under review is a collection of Agda presentations, examples, taxonomy, and scoped counterexamples. Entropy summaries, the selected shallow linear-logic encoding, and particular resource measures can discard fibre information. These examples establish limits of those encodings, not separation from Σ-types or all possible neighbouring formalisms. Novelty and priority remain unresolved research obligations.

`ResidueCell.once-only` is a read-after-consume equation on the returned spent value; ordinary Agda values remain duplicable. It does not enforce single use of an original holding value. No refinement theorem connecting these Agda models to the Rust or Idris runtimes has been established here. Thermodynamic zero-cost results concern the defined arithmetic cost model, not measured physical energy or a derivation of physical thermodynamics.
