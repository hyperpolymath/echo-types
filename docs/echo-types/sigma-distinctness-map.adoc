[IMPORTANT]
====
*Foundation scope correction, 2026-11-06.* The current contract is link:what-echo-is.adoc[the standard dependent fibre and its scoped interfaces]. Earlier “modality” means a preorder-indexed diagram, and historical “no-section” negatives mean no left inverse. The parallel-arrow example refutes arbitrary-arrow independence, not chosen-composite functoriality. Fibre-composition inverses do not establish a tropical graded monad; erasure obstructions do not ban every graded comonad. Selected lossy-encoding examples do not separate Echo from general Σ-types, and novelty/priority remain unresolved. Build counts and green receipts below are dated records, not certification of local edits; see link:../audit/2026-11-06-evidence.adoc[the audit evidence]. These qualifications supersede broader formulations below.
====

[.lead]
This file is the reviewer-facing companion to
`core/skepticisms/is-this-just-sigma-types.md`. Its job is one thing:
collect the in-repo constructions around `Echo f y`, which is definitionally `Σ A (λ x → f x ≡ y)`. None of the rows separates Echo from general dependent pairs. Selected lossy representations can forget information; that is a statement about those representations.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Statements below stay inside the *narrowed* claims of
`docs/retractions.adoc` R-2026-05-18. In particular: no "full
categorical universal property" (the pullback is funext-relative and
pointwise), no "graded comonad" (the loss structure is a thin-poset
reindexing modality), no "model-independence" (the abstract model
interface is carrier-parametricity over a fixed grade poset).

== Five demands, five answers

Each subsection states the demand, names the in-tree artefact(s) that
answer it, and notes any narrowing relative to the stronger statement a
sceptic might want.

=== Demand 1 — Irreversibility as a theorem family

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Claim | Non-injectivity rules out a left inverse for the specified loss map. This is standard mathematics expressible with raw `Σ`; the historical no-section theorem identifiers retain their names.

| Agda |
`EchoCharacteristic.no-section-collapse`,
`EchoCharacteristic.no-section-visible`,
`EchoResidue.no-section-collapse-to-residue`,
`EchoLinear.no-section-weaken`,
`EchoOrdinal.no-section-ordinal-collapse`,
`EchoEpistemicResidue.no-section-to-epistemic`. All pinned in
`proofs/agda/Smoke.agda`.

| Prose | `docs/characteristic.adoc`,
`docs/proof-obligations.md` §"No-section" entries,
`README.md` §"Characteristic family".

| Narrowing | These are no-left-inverse results, not a general absence of categorical sections or a separation from dependent pairs. The family lemmas are negative results under
`--safe --without-K`.
|===

=== Demand 2 — Algebra of loss (down-admissible, up-blocked)

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Claim | The chosen family has an indexed action over the concrete loss order. Selected non-injective down-steps have no left inverse; no statement about every possible up-map is made.

| Down-step (Agda) |
`EchoGraded.degrade-compose` and `EchoGraded.degrade-via-join` are the
per-decoration composition laws. The same recipe lands at
`EchoLinear.degradeMode-compose`, `EchoChoreo.applyChoreo-compose`,
`EchoAccess` (graded access), `EchoCost` (cost-indexed),
`EchoSearch` (witness-search).

| Up-step blocked (Agda) | `EchoLinear.no-section-weaken` (=
`EchoResidue.no-section-collapse-to-residue`).

| Separating model | `EchoSeparating.sep-degrade-compose-fails` —
the distinct parallel actions refute equality with an arbitrary direct arrow. Chosen-composite action laws remain valid. Thinness is sufficient for identifying parallel arrows, not necessary for every functorial action.

| Prose | `docs/characteristic.adoc` (Gate 2 audit), N1–N4 nominees.

| Narrowing | This interface is a preorder-indexed diagram, not a reflective modality or a graded-comonad interface. Separate writer-carrier constructions have their own laws and naturality obligations.
|===

=== Demand 3 — Categorical identity and bridges

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Claim | Echo is a recognisable categorical object (the homotopy
fibre) and other "loss-tracking" frameworks bridge into it.

| Identity (Agda) | `EchoFiberBridge.echo↔fib` — definitional fibre
identity with `refl` round-trips (Pillar A, landed 2026-05-17).

| Pullback (Agda) | `EchoPullback.echo-pullback-univ` — pullback of
`f` along `y : ⊤ → B` with *pointwise, funext-relative* mediator
property. See retraction note at the head of the module.

| Bridges (Agda) | `EchoJanusBridge.agda` (reversible debugger),
`EchoTropical.agda` + `AntiEchoTropical.agda` (tropical / argmin),
`EchoCNOBridge.agda`, `DyadicEchoBridge.agda`,
`EchoFiberCount.agda` + `EchoThermodynamicsFinite.agda` (finite-fibre
Landauer / Bennett).

| Prose | `docs/echo-types/establishment-plan.adoc` §"Pillar A",
`docs/adjacency/`.

| Narrowing | The bridges are independent witnesses, not a single
"every loss-tracker maps uniquely into Echo" theorem. Such a uniqueness
result needs a specified category and equality on homs. Pointwise hom-setoid equality avoids a global funext axiom; no universal property follows merely from the word “bridge”.
|===

=== Demand 4 — Abstraction barrier (consumer-side)

[cols="1,3", options="header"]
|===
| Claim | Consumers of the selected contractible affine residue cannot distinguish its erased sources. Retaining the source in a raw Σ would permit `proj₁`; a Σ encoding of the same erased interface has the same restriction. This is a comparison of chosen carriers.

| In-tree precursor (Agda) | `EchoLinear.affine-canonical`,
`EchoLinear.affine-all-equal` — the affine residue carrier is
contractible.

| Planned (Agda) | `proofs/agda/EchoAbstractionBarrier.agda` — Track B
in the work plan. Headline lemmas:
`affine-consumer-cannot-distinguish` (positive) and
`sigma-distinguishes` (the raw-`Σ` counter-program, as a checked
exhibit of what the barrier blocks).

| Model-side counterpart (Agda) | `EchoRelModel.GradedLossModel` +
generic `GCLaws` — carrier-parametricity over a fixed grade poset
(Pillar D, narrowed per R-2026-05-18).

| Narrowing | The planned consumer-side theorem is a *free theorem at
the headline affine instance*, not full Reynolds parametricity (Agda
has no parametricity primitive; a fully general statement would need
an axiom).
|===

== What this file does NOT claim

The five sections above are an *evidence map*, not a re-litigation of
the gate decisions. In particular:

* This file does not claim Gate 1, Gate 2, or Gate 3 is passed. That
  is the job of `roadmap-gates.adoc`, `docs/characteristic.adoc`, and
  the example modules themselves.
* This file does not claim the planned Track B / Track C work is
  landed. Where a row mentions "Planned (Agda)", the artefact does not
  yet exist on `main`; check `git log` before relying on it.
* This file does not introduce new theorems. It cross-references
  artefacts that already live elsewhere in the tree.
