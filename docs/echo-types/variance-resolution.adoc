= Echo variance — bounded findings
:toc: macro

== Status, 2026-11-06

The earlier title “resolution” overstated the result. `EchoVariance.agda` proves fibre-composition maps and their two pointwise inverse equations for a fixed supplied factorisation. The same source proves a concrete impossibility of exact bare recovery after Boolean collapse and inverse maps for the experimental writer carrier.

These statements do not assemble a tropical graded monad. That requires specified endofunctors, action on maps, unit and multiplication naturality, coherent grading, and the monad equations. The total completion `A ↔ Σ B (Echo f)` retains an `A` witness and has no grade parameter. It does not designate a grade-zero recoverable subobject or a minimal complement.

== Recovery directions

`no-bare-recovery` uses the historically named `no-section-of-collapsing-map`: its equation is `raise (collapse b) ≡ b`, a *left inverse*. The map `collapse : Bool → ⊤` does have categorical sections, namely the two constant maps choosing a Boolean. Positive section/retraction equations for the fibre equivalence and writer maps retain their stated directions and must not be renamed indiscriminately.

== Comonadic claims

A counit that recovers arbitrary content from an erasing carrier is obstructed under the specified assumptions. This is a carrier-specific claim. It does not establish a blanket impossibility of graded comonads involving loss. The experimental writer carrier stores the original content and therefore evades that erasure premise; the `A × ⊤` carrier is also content-preserving.

The baseline `GradedComonadStructure` interface omits naturality. A record inhabitant proves its declared fields only. The local `NaturalGradedComonad` companion and explicit F1/natural/list witnesses now pass Agda 2.7.0.1 checks. `EchoErasingGradedComonad` supplies an accepted natural graded-comonad counterexample with genuine positive-grade erasure; its two ordered compatibility proofs carry the documented Cubical transport computation warnings. Neither an experimental record nor fibre-composition inverses alone settle all variance or tropical-law questions.

== Index action

`ThinPoset` is abstractly a thin preorder because its fields omit antisymmetry. `EchoModality` is a preorder-indexed diagram, with a stronger formulation of composition that identifies any direct parallel arrow with the chosen composite. `EchoSeparating` refutes that arbitrary-arrow identification when parallel arrows act differently; ordinary functor composition is not refuted.
