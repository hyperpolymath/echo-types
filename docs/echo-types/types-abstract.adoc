== Object and scope

For `f : A → B`, define `Echo f y := Σ (x : A) , (f x ≡ y)`. This is the standard dependent fibre. It introduces no new judgment, formation rule, or substructural discipline. An inhabitant retains a specific source and its equality witness. The total-space equivalence `A ↔ Σ B (Echo f)` is standard; it contains the source and proves neither recovery from the bare output nor minimal residue storage.

The additional `EchoModality` interface is a diagram of types over an index preorder. The compatibility name `ThinPoset` denotes a record with reflexivity, transitivity, and propositional homs, but no antisymmetry field. The concrete three-grade instance is an order. This interface has no reflector, reflective-subuniverse predicate, idempotence, or modal elimination principle.

== Mechanized presentations

. *Fibre calibration.* `EchoFiberBridge.echo↔fib` is a definitional comparison with a restatement of the fibre. `EchoFiberTriangulation` compares standard-library surjection witnesses and opposite equality orientations. Neither is a soundness metatheorem.
. *Pullback presentation.* `EchoPullback` supplies a pointwise mediator. Function-level strengthenings in F4/F5 take funext explicitly. Pointwise equality can instead serve as hom-setoid equality; its use does not require a global funext axiom.
. *Indexed action.* `EchoGraded` and the public interface supply identity and composition equations for degradation over the selected index. The legacy `gextract`, `gduplicate`, and `gcomonad-*` names in `EchoGradedComonad` describe single-index coercions and their equations, not nested graded-comonad structure.
. *Parallel-arrow counterexample.* `EchoSeparating` acts differently on two parallel arrows. It refutes independence from an arbitrary direct arrow while preserving identity and composition with the chosen composite. This is no general separation from Σ-types or a failure of functor composition.
. *Source-retaining completion and classification.* `EchoTotalCompletion`, `EchoLossTaxonomy`, `EchoResidueTaxonomy`, `EchoDecorationStructure`, and `EchoObservationalEquivalence` organize standard constructions and selected examples.
. *Exact recovery obstruction.* The historical `no-section-of-collapsing-map` identifier rules out a left inverse of a non-injective map. Boolean collapse to unit has two right inverses. Positive section/retraction laws for equivalences retain their actual direction.
. *Conditional factorization results.* F5 supplies a strict triangle, diagonal lifting, and factorization uniqueness up to isomorphism with its explicit parameters. These statements alone do not establish unique diagonal fillers; no unqualified orthogonal-factorization-system theorem follows from those names.

`EchoVariance` supplies inverse maps between a composite fibre and an iterated fibre for a chosen factorization. This does not assemble a tropical graded monad: endofunctors, naturality, grading coherence, and all monad laws require a separate specification. An erasing-carrier recovery obstruction is carrier-specific, and does not prohibit every loss-related graded comonad. The baseline `GradedComonadStructure` omits naturality; record inhabitants prove only the declared fields. The local `NaturalGradedComonad` companion strengthens that contract, and it and its F1/natural/list witnesses pass the recorded local checks. A natural erasing graded-comonad counterexample also passes; its ordered compatibility proofs carry the documented Cubical transport warnings.

== Selected comparisons

`EchoEntropy`, `EchoLLEncoding`, and resource-measure examples show information loss in particular chosen observations or encodings. They do not distinguish Echo from general dependent pairs, all linear-logic representations, or all resource algebras. A dependent-pair encoding can express exactly the same fibre invariant. Novelty and priority of the presentation remain unresolved research obligations.

The relevant literature includes the HoTT treatment of fibres; graded monads and coeffects (Katsumata; Fujii, Katsumata and Melliès; Petriček, Orchard and Mycroft; Orchard, Liepelt and Eades); comonadic computation (Uustalu and Vene); quantitative type theory (Atkey); and lenses/optics, refinement types, provenance, and modal type theories. These are comparisons to established structures, not evidence that this library implements all their interfaces or rules.

The linear/affine examples model weakening equations. They do not prevent duplication of an Agda witness. `ResidueCell.once-only` proves reading the returned consumed cell yields `nothing`; the original value remains reusable. No Rust/Idris runtime refinement has been established here. Thermodynamic equations concern the defined arithmetic cost model, not physical energy measurements.

== CNO and categorical follow-up

Unrestricted equivalence between `Echo p s` and `Echo id s` characterizes invertible fibre shape, not pointwise identity: Boolean negation is the counterexample. A source-preserving comparison supplies the missing constraint. The baseline constant `Program → ⊤` bridge ignored its program index and CNO certificate.

Local follow-ups `EchoSourcePreserving`, `EchoCNOSemantics`, and `EchoDependentPushforward` address source preservation, native `CNO.state-eq`, and dependent pushforward/reindexing with pointwise hom-setoids. Their local Agda 2.7.0.1 checks pass. The ordinary fibre-isomorphism characterization includes inverse laws; the native CNO criterion instead uses a source-preserving map of `state-eq`-valued fibres. The CNO characterization is scoped to the pinned dependency's total, zero-energy execution model. The pushforward composition natural isomorphism does not establish full base-indexed coherence. No new linear runtime or ordered tropical programme is implied.
