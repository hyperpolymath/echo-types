= Proof-witness audit: Echo Types and Mathesis

Audit date: 2026-11-01. Echo Types source base:
`b0982c44f36133acd2d0e1d4e03cf031fff7a470` (working tree also has
uncommitted CI/documentation edits). This is a source and receipt inspection,
not a new theorem-checking receipt. No proof was translated or rewritten.

== Policy and scope

Mathesis `estate-mathematical-architecture` distinguishes a locally
checked Agda result (Grade 3), a witness in one alternative foundational
system (Grade 2i), translations in two distinct foundational families
(Grade 2ii), and checking translated witnesses through the specified
headless checker APIs (Grade 1). Agda, Lean and Rocq all belong to the DTT
family; Lean plus Rocq alone is not foundational diversity for Grade 2ii.
Grades attach to specific results and evidence, not entire repositories.
The original repositories own their proofs. Missing evidence remains missing.

== Findings

* This checkout contains no `.lean` or `.v` files. A filesystem search
  does not rule out witnesses in other repositories.
* Candidate Rocq/Coq witness: ephapax
  https://github.com/hyperpolymath/ephapax/blob/800030f3c92536cce57acb5e82da6cf27b942826/formal/Echo.v[`formal/Echo.v`]
  declares `no_section_collapse_to_residue` and describes it as a mirror
  of `proofs/agda/EchoResidue.agda`'s `no-section-collapse-to-residue`.
  Both source statements concern nonexistence of a section after the
  concrete Boolean collapse. The source also contains `weaken`,
  `weaken_collapses_distinction`, `affine_canonical`, `mode_le_prop`, and
  `degrade_mode_comp`. This is a candidate *single-system translation*,
  not independent corroboration or a checked cross-language equivalence.
  The source claims zero axioms and K-freedom; this audit did not run
  `Print Assumptions` for these specific theorems and cannot certify that
  claim from source inspection alone.
* Ephapax's `_CoqProject` at that revision includes `Echo.v`, but its
  https://github.com/hyperpolymath/ephapax/actions/runs/40435929887[successful
  workflow run] at the same revision skipped the actual Coq build job.
  Its green aggregate is therefore not a compilation receipt. The
  workflow's `Print Assumptions` step names four other preservation
  theorems, not the Echo witness above. No coq-lsp REST API receipt
  for this witness was located in this inspection.
* Eclexia
  https://github.com/hyperpolymath/eclexia/blob/742cc99cca37f1bda84d8d9188cb4f61fff4341e/formal/coq/src/EchoThermo.v[`EchoThermo.v`]
  contains a discrete thermodynamic model and explicitly claims to mirror
  `EchoThermodynamics`; its scope excludes the real-valued energy scaling.
  Treat this as an adjacent candidate correspondence, not automatically
  the same theorem or a verified translation. No theorem-specific build,
  assumptions, or checker-API receipt was examined for it.
* `docs/bridges/tropical-correspondence.adoc` describes Lean and Isabelle
  neighbours, but explicitly notes different carriers, no shared definition
  module and no cross-prover checker. Similar names or informal analogy
  alone do not establish witness identity or a grade.

== Disposition

No Grade 1, Grade 2ii, or verified Grade 2i receipt is established by this
inspection. The ephapax source is a concrete Grade 2i *candidate*; its
translation correspondence, exact source commits, theorem assumptions and
successful checker receipt need review before recording a grade. Agda was unavailable when this witness audit was performed. A subsequent
local cold check is recorded in `PROOF-STATUS.adoc`; it does not by itself
prove a cross-language correspondence or provide a clean-commit receipt. In particular, these candidates do not cover the experimental
or bit-narrowing modules merely by existing elsewhere. Do not infer any
regression solely from an absent receipt.
